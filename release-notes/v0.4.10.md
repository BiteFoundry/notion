## What's Changed

### Modular Architecture & Terminal Safety
- **Terminal & Agent Safe-Output (`src/safe_text.rs`)**: Sanitized untrusted Notion page, database, block, and comment text rendered to CLI terminal output against ANSI/VT100 escape sequences (CSI/OSC/DCS/C1), C0/C1 control characters, Unicode Bidi overrides, and invisible Unicode Tag characters (`U+E0000..=U+E007F`), while preserving raw `--json` output.
- **Supply Chain & Packaging**: Added `[package.metadata.binstall]` for `cargo-binstall`, `deny.toml` + `.github/workflows/audit.yml` (`cargo-deny` CI), and `refs/tags/v` tag guards on publish/release jobs.
- **Codebase Modularization**: Decomposed `src/cli.rs`, `src/ui/action.rs` (`action::dispatch` 2,163L → 120L), `src/ui/app.rs` (`handle_ui_msg` 547L → 127L), and all 3 E2E suites (`mock_notion_e2e`, `read_contract_e2e`, `feedback_regression_e2e`) into focused domain submodules.

## What's Changed

### Binary Size, Build Diet & Sync/Editor Hardening
- **Smaller Release Binaries**: Enabled `opt-level = "s"` and `panic = "abort"` in `[profile.release]`, cutting release binary sizes by ~40% with zero feature loss.
- **Sync & Page-Edit Hardening**: Eliminated Mutex-poison `unwrap()` panics in `src/sync.rs` and hardened `src/page_edit.rs` / `src/cli.rs` invariants, boxed oversized enum variants (`Overlay`, `ActiveOutcome`), removed dead canvas shim code, and resolved all Clippy warnings (`-D warnings` clean).
- **Dependency & Packaging Diet**: Upgraded `ratatui` to `0.29`, `dirs` to `6`, and trimmed `bitefoundry-html` `resvg` features; removed unused `crossterm/event-stream`, `reqwest/stream`, `futures`, and `uuid`, moved `tempfile` to dev-dependencies, added optional Cargo feature gates (`html-js`, `svg-graphics`, `desktop-notify`), and externalized `assets/demo.gif` to `bitefoundry.dev`.

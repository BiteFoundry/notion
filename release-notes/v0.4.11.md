## What's Changed

### Performance Optimization Suite (SQLite/FTS5, Async Concurrency, TUI Cache & Pixel Pipeline)
- **SQLite/FTS5 & HTTP Sync**: Upgraded to a 4-reader WAL pool, wired `prepare_cached`, migrated `pages_fts` to `rowid`-indexed deletes (`O(N²) → O(log N)`), eliminated `search_local` N+1 hydration via SQL `LEFT JOIN`, offloaded `serve.rs` storage calls to `spawn_blocking`, tuned `reqwest` pooling/keepalive/`tcp_nodelay`, and replaced chunked `join_all` barriers with bounded `buffer_unordered(3)`.
- **TUI Render & Caching**: Switched sidebar/DB sorting to `sort_by_cached_key`, added `Arc<[SidebarItem]>` and `Arc<PageCanvasScene>` generation-keyed caches, replaced O(H×L) inline hit scans with `partition_point`, fixed O(K²) paste-burst detection, and eliminated 2-pass `html_preview` cache thrashing.
- **Pixel Engine**: Upgraded Sixel quantization to flat 32K histogram + 5-5-5 LUT + 2-row `i16` dithering + fast ASCII RLE, switched PNG encoding to `Fast`/`NoFilter` (`fdeflate`), and enabled `opt-level = 3` for codec/pixel crates.
